✎Problems

  1. (§4.1) Let \(f(x) = 9 - x^2\) on \([0, 3]\). (a) Compute \(L_6\), \(R_6\) and \(M_6\). (b) Sketch the graph with the rectangles for \(R_6\). (c) Which of \(L_6\) and \(R_6\) is an overestimate, and why?

  2. (§4.1, applied) A car accelerates from rest. Its speedometer is read every 10 seconds:

    \(t\) (s)0102030405060
    \(v\) (m/s)081418212324

    (a) Find lower and upper estimates for the distance travelled in the first minute. (b) Estimate the distance with the Midpoint Rule using three intervals of 20 seconds.

  3. (§4.2) Express the limit \(\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{2}{n}\left(1 + \frac{2i}{n}\right)^3\) as a definite integral on a suitable interval, then evaluate it using FTC Part 2.

  4. (§4.2) Use the definition of the definite integral with right endpoints and the summation formulas to evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_0^2 (3x^2 - x + 1)\,dx\). Then check your answer with FTC Part 2.

  5. (§4.2) Evaluate by interpreting each integral in terms of areas (no antiderivatives): (a) \(\displaystyle\int_{-2}^{2}\left(1 + \sqrt{4 - x^2}\right)dx\)   (b) \(\displaystyle\int_0^5 |x - 2|\,dx\).

  6. (§4.2) (a) Given \(\int_0^4 f(x)\,dx = 5\), \(\int_0^6 f(x)\,dx = 9\) and \(\int_4^6 g(x)\,dx = -2\), find \(\int_4^6 \big[3f(x) - 2g(x)\big]\,dx\) and \(\int_6^4 f(x)\,dx\). (b) Prove, using a property of integrals (do not evaluate the integral), that \(\displaystyle 2 \le \int_0^1 \sqrt{4 + x^3}\,dx \le \sqrt{5}\).

  7. (§4.3) (a) Find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) if \(\displaystyle y = \int_{\cos x}^{x^2} \sqrt{1 + t^4}\,dt\). (b) Find a function \(f\) and a number \(a\) such that \(\displaystyle\int_a^x f(t)\,dt = x^3 - 8\) for all \(x\).

  8. (§4.3) Evaluate: (a) \(\displaystyle\int_1^4 \frac{2 + x^2}{\sqrt{x}}\,dx\)   (b) \(\displaystyle\int_{\pi/6}^{\pi/3} \csc x\cot x\,dx\).

  9. (§4.4) Find the general indefinite integral and check your answer by differentiation: \(\displaystyle\int \left(\sin x + 2\sec x\tan x - \frac{1}{x^3}\right)dx\).

  10. (§4.4, applied) A particle moving on a line has acceleration \(a(t) = 2t + 1\) m/s² and initial velocity \(v(0) = -6\) m/s. (a) Find \(v(t)\). (b) Find the displacement and (c) the total distance travelled during \(0 \le t \le 4\).

  11. (§4.4, applied) A tank holds 1000 L of water at \(t = 0\). Water flows in at a rate of \(r(t) = 200 - 4t\) litres per minute for \(0 \le t \le 30\). (a) What does \(\int_0^{30} r(t)\,dt\) represent? (b) How much water is in the tank after 30 minutes? (c) Explain why the amount of water is still increasing at \(t = 30\).

  12. (§4.5) Find: (a) \(\displaystyle\int x^2\sin(x^3 + 1)\,dx\)   (b) \(\displaystyle\int \frac{2x + 3}{(x^2 + 3x + 1)^3}\,dx\)   (c) \(\displaystyle\int x^3\sqrt{x^2 + 1}\,dx\).

  13. (§4.5) Evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_0^3 \frac{x}{\sqrt{x + 1}}\,dx\) in two ways: (a) by changing the limits of integration, and (b) by finding the indefinite integral first and using the original limits.

  14. (§4.5, prove) (a) Prove that if \(f\) is continuous and odd, then \(\int_{-a}^{a} f(x)\,dx = 0\). (Hint: split the integral at 0 and substitute \(u = -x\) in one piece.) (b) Use symmetry to evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_{-\pi/3}^{\pi/3}\left(x^5\cos x + \sec^2 x\right)dx\).

  15. (§4.5, challenge) Suppose \(f\) is continuous and \(\int_0^4 f(x)\,dx = 10\). Find (a) \(\displaystyle\int_0^2 f(2x)\,dx\) and (b) \(\displaystyle\int_0^1 x\,f(4x^2)\,dx\). Explain each substitution you use.

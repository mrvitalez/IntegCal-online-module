✎Problems

  1. Find the exact length of the curve \(y = 1 + 6x^{3/2}\) for \(0 \le x \le 1\).

  2. Find the length of \(y = \dfrac{x^4}{8} + \dfrac{1}{4x^2}\) for \(1 \le x \le 2\).

  3. Find the length of the curve \(x = \dfrac{y^2}{2} - \dfrac{\ln y}{4}\) for \(1 \le y \le 2\).

  4. Find the length of \(y = \ln(\cos x)\) for \(0 \le x \le \pi/4\).

  5. Find the arc length function \(s(x)\) for \(y = \dfrac{x^2}{2} - \dfrac{\ln x}{4}\) with starting point \(\bigl(1, \tfrac12\bigr)\). Use it to find the length of the curve from \(x = 1\) to \(x = 3\).

  6. Set up an integral for the length of one arch of \(y = \sin x\) (\(0 \le x \le \pi\)). Explain why it cannot be evaluated by the methods of this course, and approximate it with Simpson's Rule using \(n = 6\) or with a calculator, to three decimal places.

  7. The curve \(y = \sqrt{1 + 4x}\), \(1 \le x \le 5\), is rotated about the \(x\)-axis. Find the exact area of the surface.

  8. The curve \(x = \tfrac13(y^2 + 2)^{3/2}\), \(0 \le y \le 1\), is rotated about the \(x\)-axis. Find the area of the surface. (Hint: use \(ds\) in terms of \(dy\).)

  9. (Prove) A right circular cone has base radius \(r\) and height \(h\). By rotating the segment \(y = \tfrac{r}{h}x\), \(0 \le x \le h\), about the \(x\)-axis, show that its lateral surface area is \(\pi r\sqrt{r^2 + h^2}\). Then show that the incorrect formula \(\int_0^h 2\pi y\,dx\) gives \(\pi r h\), and explain geometrically why it is too small.

  10. Let \(f(x) = k(4x - x^2)\) for \(0 \le x \le 4\) and \(f(x) = 0\) otherwise. (a) Find \(k\) so that \(f\) is a pdf. (b) Find \(P(X < 1)\). (c) Find the mean of \(X\) and explain why your answer is reasonable.

  11. (Applied) The time between buses at a stop is exponentially distributed with mean 10 minutes. (a) Find the probability that you wait at most 5 minutes. (b) Find the median waiting time. (c) Prove that for the density \(f(t) = ce^{-ct}\) (\(t \ge 0\), \(c > 0\)) the mean is \(1/c\).

  12. (Applied) Heights of students in a large class are normally distributed with mean 165 cm and standard deviation 8 cm. Write the integral that gives the probability that a randomly chosen student is between 155 cm and 175 cm tall, and evaluate it numerically (Simpson's Rule with \(n = 10\), or a calculator) to three decimal places.

  13. Let \(f(x) = \dfrac{2}{(1 + x)^3}\) for \(x \ge 0\) and \(f(x) = 0\) for \(x < 0\). (a) Show that \(f\) is a pdf. (b) Find its cumulative distribution function \(F(x)\) for \(x \ge 0\). (c) Find the median. (d) Find the mean.

📝Problem Set 3: Techniques of Integration

This problem set covers integration by parts, trigonometric integrals, trigonometric substitution, partial fractions, strategy for integration and improper integrals (Stewart §§7.1–7.5 and 7.8). Solve each problem in your notebook and show every step: name the method you use, state your choice of \(u\) and \(dv\) or your substitution, and for improper integrals write the limit explicitly. Check indefinite integrals by differentiating your answer. Problems 13–15 are optional and use the special-topic module on the Laplace and Fourier transforms.

✎Problems

  1. (§7.1) Use integration by parts. (a) Find \(\displaystyle\int x\sec^2 x\,dx\). (b) Use the tabular method to evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi}x^2\sin x\,dx\). (c) Let \(I = \displaystyle\int e^{-x}\sin 2x\,dx\). Apply integration by parts twice and solve for \(I\).

  2. (§7.1, prove) (a) Use integration by parts to prove the reduction formula \(\displaystyle\int x^n e^x\,dx = x^n e^x - n\int x^{n-1}e^x\,dx\). (b) Apply it three times to find \(\displaystyle\int x^3e^x\,dx\). (c) Explain, using LIATE, why the choice of \(u\) you made in part (a) is the natural one.

  3. (§7.2) Find \(\displaystyle\int\sin^5 x\cos^2 x\,dx\).

  4. (§7.2) Evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/3}\tan^3 x\sec x\,dx\).

  5. (§7.2, applied) An alternating current in a circuit is \(I(t) = I_0\sin(120\pi t)\), where \(t\) is in seconds. The average of \(I^2\) over one cycle, \(\displaystyle 60\int_0^{1/60} I(t)^2\,dt\), determines the power delivered. Show that this average equals \(\tfrac12 I_0^2\), and hence that the \"root-mean-square\" current is \(I_0/\sqrt2\).

  6. (§7.3) Use the substitution \(x = 3\sec\theta\) to find \(\displaystyle\int\frac{\sqrt{x^2 - 9}}{x}\,dx\) for \(x \gt 3\). Draw the reference triangle you used.

  7. (§7.3, prove) (a) Use trigonometric substitution to prove that the area enclosed by the ellipse \(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} + \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1\) is \(\pi ab\). (b) A garden bed has the shape of the ellipse \(\dfrac{x^2}{16} + \dfrac{y^2}{9} = 1\), with \(x\) and \(y\) in metres. How many square metres of soil cover does it need?

  8. (§7.4) Evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_2^3\frac{x^2 + 3}{x^3 - x}\,dx\).

  9. (§7.4) Find \(\displaystyle\int\frac{x^3 + 2x^2 + 6x + 4}{x^2 + 2x + 5}\,dx\). (Hint: divide first, then complete the square.)

  10. (§7.5) No method is given; choose one and explain your choice. (a) \(\displaystyle\int\frac{\sqrt{x}}{1 + x}\,dx\) (b) \(\displaystyle\int\frac{x}{\sqrt{1 - x^4}}\,dx\).

  11. (§7.8, applied) The lifetime \(T\) (in years) of a certain battery has probability density \(f(t) = 0.5e^{-0.5t}\) for \(t \ge 0\). (a) Show that \(\displaystyle\int_0^{\infty}f(t)\,dt = 1\). (b) The mean lifetime is \(\displaystyle\int_0^{\infty}t f(t)\,dt\). Compute it, writing each improper integral as a limit.

  12. (§7.8) Decide whether each integral converges or diverges, and evaluate it if it converges. Justify each answer. (a) \(\displaystyle\int_0^1\ln x\,dx\) (b) \(\displaystyle\int_0^2\frac{dx}{x - 1}\) (c) \(\displaystyle\int_1^{\infty}\frac{dx}{x^3 + x + 1}\) (convergence only; use the Comparison Test).

  13. (Optional, special topic) The Laplace transform of \(f\) is \(\mathcal{L}\{f\}(s) = \displaystyle\int_0^{\infty}e^{-st}f(t)\,dt\). Use this definition, integration by parts and a limit to show that \(\mathcal{L}\{te^{2t}\}(s) = \dfrac{1}{(s - 2)^2}\) for \(s \gt 2\). Why is the condition \(s \gt 2\) needed?

  14. (Optional, special topic) Use Laplace transforms, with \(\mathcal{L}\{y'\} = sY(s) - y(0)\) and \(\mathcal{L}\{e^{at}\} = \frac{1}{s-a}\), to solve \(y' + 2y = e^{-t}\), \(y(0) = 1\). Use partial fractions to invert \(Y(s)\), then check your answer in the differential equation.

  15. (Optional, special topic) The Fourier transform of \(f\) is \(F(\omega) = \displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(t)e^{-i\omega t}\,dt\). Find the Fourier transform of the rectangular pulse \(f(t) = 3\) for \(|t| \le 2\) and \(f(t) = 0\) otherwise. Write your answer using \(\sin\), and find \(F(0)\). What does \(F(0)\) represent?

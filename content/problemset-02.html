✎Problems

  1. Find the area of the region enclosed by \(y = x^2 - 2x\) and \(y = x + 4\).

  2. Find the total area between \(y = \cos x\) and \(y = \sin 2x\) on \([0, \pi/2]\). (The curves cross inside the interval.)

  3. Sketch the region enclosed by \(x = 2y^2\) and \(x = y^2 + 4\), and find its area by integrating with respect to \(y\).

  4. Find the total area of the regions enclosed by \(y = x^3 - x\) and \(y = 3x\).

  5. The region under \(y = e^x\), \(0 \le x \le 1\), is rotated about the \(x\)-axis. Find the volume.

  6. The region bounded by \(y = x^2\) and \(y = 4\) is rotated about the line \(y = -1\). Find the volume of the solid.

  7. The base of a solid is the region between \(y = 1 - x^2\) and the \(x\)-axis. Cross-sections perpendicular to the \(x\)-axis are equilateral triangles with one side in the base. Find the volume.

  8. Use cylindrical shells to find the volume of the solid obtained by rotating the region under \(y = \sin(x^2)\), \(0 \le x \le \sqrt{\pi}\), about the \(y\)-axis. Explain why the washer method would be much harder here.

  9. The region bounded by \(y = x^2\), \(y = 0\) and \(x = 2\) is rotated about the line \(x = 3\). Find the volume using shells.

  10. A spring has natural length 0.2 m. It takes 0.5 J of work to stretch it from its natural length to a length of 0.3 m. How much work is needed to stretch it from a length of 0.3 m to a length of 0.4 m?

  11. (Applied) A vertical cylindrical water tank has radius 3 m and height 6 m and is filled to a depth of 4 m. Find the work required to pump all the water out through a pipe whose outlet is 1 m above the top of the tank.

  12. (Applied) A 12 m chain with mass 3 kg/m hangs from a winch at the top of a scaffold, and a 20 kg load is attached to its lower end. How much work is needed to wind the whole chain, with the load, up to the winch?

  13. Find the average value of \(f(x) = \dfrac{1}{1 + x^2}\) on \([0, 1]\), and find a number \(c\) in \([0,1]\) at which \(f(c) = f_{\text{ave}}\).

  14. (Explain/prove) Use the slicing method to show that a pyramid with a square base of side \(b\) and height \(h\) has volume \(\tfrac13 b^2 h\). Then explain in your own words why the washer area is \(\pi(R^2 - r^2)\) rather than \(\pi(R - r)^2\).

  15. (Prove) Show that for any linear function \(f(x) = px + q\), the average value of \(f\) on \([a, b]\) equals \(f\bigl(\tfrac{a+b}{2}\bigr)\). What does this say about the number \(c\) in the Mean Value Theorem for Integrals for a linear function?

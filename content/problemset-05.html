✎Problems

  1. (§10.1) Sketch the curve \(x = 1 + 2t,\ y = 4 - t^2\), \(-2 \le t \le 3\), marking the initial and terminal points and the direction of motion. Then eliminate the parameter to find a Cartesian equation.

  2. (§10.1) Eliminate the parameter from \(x = 2\sec t,\ y = 3\tan t\), \(-\pi/2 \lt t \lt \pi/2\). Identify the curve, state which part of it is traced, and give the direction of motion.

  3. (§10.3) (a) Convert the polar point \((-2, \pi/3)\) to Cartesian coordinates. (b) Give polar coordinates with \(r \gt 0\), \(0 \le \theta \lt 2\pi\) for the Cartesian point \((2, -2\sqrt3)\). (c) Identify the curve \(r = \dfrac{2}{\cos\theta + \sin\theta}\). (d) Write \(x^2 - y^2 = 1\) in polar form.

  4. (§10.2) Find an equation of the tangent line to \(x = t^3 - t,\ y = t^2 + 2t\) at the point where \(t = 1\).

  5. (§10.2) Find all points on \(x = t^3 - 12t,\ y = t^2 - 4\) where the tangent line is horizontal or vertical.

  6. (§10.2) Consider \(x = t^2,\ y = t^3 - 3t\). (a) Find \(dy/dx\) and \(d^2y/dx^2\). (b) For which \(t\) is the curve concave upward? (c) The curve crosses itself at one point and forms a loop. Find that point and the area enclosed by the loop.

  7. (§10.2) Find the exact length of the curve \(x = \cos t + t\sin t,\ y = \sin t - t\cos t\), \(0 \le t \le 2\pi\).

  8. (§10.3) Sketch the cardioid \(r = 1 - \cos\theta\), stating its symmetry. Find the points where the tangent line is horizontal.

  9. (§10.4) (a) Find the area enclosed by one loop of the lemniscate \(r^2 = 9\cos 2\theta\). (b) Find the area of the region that lies inside both circles \(r = 2\sin\theta\) and \(r = 2\cos\theta\).

  10. (§10.4) Find the total length of the cardioid \(r = 3(1 + \cos\theta)\).

  11. (§10.5 review) For each conic find the vertices, foci and eccentricity, and for the hyperbola the asymptotes: (a) \(\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1\); (b) \(9y^2 - 16x^2 = 144\).

  12. (§10.6) (a) Identify \(r = \dfrac{15}{3 + 3\cos\theta}\) and give its directrix and vertex. (b) Identify \(r = \dfrac{6}{2 - \sin\theta}\); give its eccentricity, directrix, vertices and centre. (c) Write a polar equation of the conic with focus at the pole, eccentricity 3 and directrix \(y = -2\).

  13. (Applied, §10.1–10.2) A ball is kicked from ground level; its position after \(t\) seconds is \(x = 18t,\ y = 24t - 4.9t^2\) (metres). (a) When and how far away does it land? (b) Find its maximum height. (c) At what angle below the horizontal is it moving when it lands? (Use \(dy/dx\).)

  14. (Applied, §10.2) A decorative bead is shaped like a sphere of radius 3 cm with two equal caps sliced off. Its curved surface is obtained by rotating the arc \(x = 3\cos t,\ y = 3\sin t\), \(\pi/6 \le t \le 5\pi/6\), about the \(x\)-axis. Find the area of this curved surface.

  15. (Explain and prove) (a) Let the focus \(F\) be at the pole and the directrix be the line \(x = d\), \(d \gt 0\). Starting from \(|PF| = e\,|Pl|\), prove that the conic has polar equation \(r = \dfrac{ed}{1 + e\cos\theta}\). (b) A classmate computes the area of the circle \(r = 2\sin\theta\) as \(\int_0^{2\pi}\frac12(2\sin\theta)^2\,d\theta\) and gets \(2\pi\). Explain what went wrong and give the correct area.
